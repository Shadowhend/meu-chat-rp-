🎭 LobbyZero — Global RP Chat

✨ Welcome to LobbyZero!

LobbyZero is an open, global text roleplay hub created to bring back the warm, creative human connection lost with the shutdown of platforms like Project Z and Omlet Arcade. No endless channels, no strict application forms—just grab your character and write your story.

  • 🔹 Set up: Fill in your Character Name and paste any image URL for your Avatar Photo before messaging.
  • 🔹 Actions/Narration: Start and end your sentence with asterisks to format actions in italic. (e.g., *walks into the tavern*)
  • 🔹 Roll Dice: Type exactly /d20 in the message bar to roll a 20-sided dice and test your character's luck!
  • 🔹 Safety First: If someone is ruining your experience, simply click the red [Mute] button next to their name to block their texts.
