✨ Welcome to LobbyZero!

LobbyZero is an open, global text roleplay hub created to bring back the warm, creative human connection lost with the shutdown of platforms like Project Z and Omlet Arcade. No endless channels, no strict application forms—just grab your character and write your story.

  • 🔹 Set up: Fill in your Character Name and optionally paste an image URL for your Avatar Photo.
  • 🔹 Actions/Narration: Put asterisks around actions to display them in italics (e.g., *walks into the tavern*).
  • 🔹 Roll Dice: Type exactly /d20 in the message bar to roll a 20-sided die.
  • 🔹 Safety First: Click [Mute] next to a name to hide messages from that user in your current session.
